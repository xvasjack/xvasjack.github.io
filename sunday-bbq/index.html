Sungai Tua notes

  • Riverside spots go first. Aim to be parked by 8 am.
  • Small cash entry fee per person and per car. Bring coins.
  • No shops inside. Last stop for ice and forgotten items is Batu Caves or Selayang.
  • Bring your own grill and charcoal. Pits are few and often taken.
  • Rocks are slippery. Water shoes or sandals with grip.
  • Pack out every bag of rubbish.
